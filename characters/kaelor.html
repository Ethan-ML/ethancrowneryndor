Kaelor

Iron King of Arkhan

Race Human
Gender Male
Age 32 Years
Nation Arkhan Wastes
Weapon Arcsteel Saber
Magic Technomancy

Biography

Kaelor is the reigning king of the Arkhan Wastes, a melancholic ruler known for keeping both his kingdom and himself constantly on the defensive. He governs with an iron grip and rarely accepts help from others, preferring complete control over every decision that affects his nation.

Cold, guarded, and deeply distrustful, Kaelor has little patience for emotional interference. He relies heavily on strategy, machinery, and technological development, investing enormous resources into making Arkhan one of the most advanced territories in Eryndor. He believes progress and power are the only reliable ways to secure the future he wants.

Kaelor possesses a balanced combination of combat skill and technological magic. His Arcsteel Saber is enhanced with engineered energy, while his Technomancy allows him to interact with and control advanced mechanisms far beyond ordinary craftsmanship. His danger comes less from raw destructive power and more from the resources and defenses he can command as king.

Much of Kaelor's obsession with advancement is rooted in the memory of a former love he has never truly abandoned. He believes that if he pushes technology far enough, he may eventually discover a way to bring that person back. Because of this fixation, he reacts with immediate hostility toward women who approach him with romantic intentions, interpreting such advances as both an intrusion and a betrayal of the person he still hopes to recover.
