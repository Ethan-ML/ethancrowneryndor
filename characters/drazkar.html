Drazkar

Grand Dark Mage of the Suffocating Void

Race Human
Gender Male
Age 41 Years
Nation The Suffocating Void
Weapon Ravenwood Staff
Magic Dark Magic

Biography

Drazkar is a powerful dark mage who lives hidden within the Whispering Forest of the Suffocating Void. Born with an unusually strong affinity for dark magic, he developed abilities that eventually earned him a reputation as a Grand Dark Mage, forcing him to remain concealed from white mages who consider practitioners like him a threat.

Quiet and withdrawn, Drazkar rarely speaks unless necessary. Despite the frightening reputation surrounding his magic, he is naturally protective rather than cruel and has little interest in using his abilities simply to harm others. He prefers the isolation of the forest, where he can practice his magic without interference.

Both highly powerful and considerably dangerous, Drazkar possesses extensive control over dark energy. His Ravenwood Staff strengthens his spells, while his connection to shadows allows him to conceal his presence and defend himself against those who hunt him. His abilities make confronting him directly extremely risky, particularly within the darkness of his forest.

Drazkar has an unusual affection for ravens and treats the birds inhabiting the Whispering Forest with remarkable care. Many remain close to him and seem unusually comfortable in his presence, sometimes acting as his eyes beyond the places where he can safely travel. Threatening them is one of the quickest ways to provoke the otherwise reserved mage.
