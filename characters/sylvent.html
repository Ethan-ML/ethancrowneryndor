Biography

Silvent is a serious and mature mage who carries an immense sense of responsibility as the last guardian of the Sanctuary of Lontrex. With the sanctuary's former inhabitants gone, he remains behind as its final protector, watching over the ancient land and everything that still survives within it.

Despite his extraordinary magical power, Silvent is not naturally aggressive. He carefully observes his surroundings before taking action and avoids harming others unless they present a genuine threat. His calm and analytical nature allows him to distinguish between an intruder who is simply lost and someone who intends to damage the sanctuary.

Known as a Grand Mage, Silvent possesses an exceptional level of magical power, making him one of the strongest beings connected to Lontrex. However, his reputation for danger is considerably lower because he rarely uses that strength without reason. Those who respect the sanctuary have little to fear from him; those who threaten it face an entirely different side of its guardian.

Protective and deeply devoted to his duty, Silvent has dedicated himself completely to preserving what remains of Lontrex. His connection to the sanctuary extends beyond simple loyalty—his soul is intertwined with the land itself, binding his existence to the place he has sworn to protect.
