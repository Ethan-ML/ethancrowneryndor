Gemora

A kingdom forged by fire, ancient creatures and the eternal power of the volcanoes.

Discover Gemora

Overview

Gemora was shaped by volcanoes long before its cities rose between the mountains. Black stone, rivers of magma and enormous volcanic formations dominate the landscape, forcing its inhabitants to build settlements capable of surviving intense heat, falling ash and constant seismic activity.

Its architecture reflects that environment. Fortresses and cities are carved from dark volcanic rock, illuminated by the orange glow of lava flowing beneath bridges, walls and underground chambers. Fire is not simply a danger in Gemora; it is part of its identity, culture and magic.

Today the nation stands entirely under the authority of King Morzeth. From the Ember Throne, he commands Gemora and the Crimson Legion, ruling over Humans, Fireborn and Draconic Beings. His ambitions also extend beyond his own borders, making the surviving Sanctuary of Leontrex and its guardian Sylvent one of the obstacles he has yet to overcome.

Life has adapted even to Gemora's extreme environment. Dark forests grow near volcanic regions, filled with heat-resistant trees, ember-colored plants and glowing vegetation. Fire lizards, ash-covered creatures and powerful draconic beasts inhabit the mountains, while ancient magma dragons remain among the most feared creatures hidden beneath the volcanic landscape.
