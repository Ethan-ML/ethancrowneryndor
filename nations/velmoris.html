Velmoris

A kingdom surrounded by towering black mountains, eternal mist and ancient darkness, where powerful knights, mages and forgotten beasts guard the secrets hidden beneath the stone.

Discover Velmoris

Overview

Velmoris is surrounded by towering black mountains that isolate much of the kingdom from the rest of Eryndor. Thick mist moves through its valleys almost constantly, hiding roads, forests and settlements between enormous formations of dark stone.

Noctis Keep dominates the region from its position among the mountains. Its architecture mirrors the landscape: imposing fortresses, obsidian walls and dark halls illuminated by moonlight. Smaller settlements can be found throughout the valleys, inhabited by communities that have learned to survive the dangerous highlands.

The Obsidian Throne rules over Humans, Elves, Shamans and Psychics, creating a society where military strength exists alongside spiritual and magical traditions. Shadow, lunar and spiritual magic are widely practiced, while the Obsidian Legion guards the mountain roads and protects settlements from the creatures that live beyond them.

Vegetation grows mainly in sheltered valleys, where dark flowers, silver moss and hardy mountain plants survive beneath the mist. Wolves, nocturnal birds and other mountain animals inhabit the region, but the greatest danger lies underground. Deep cracks and caverns beneath the black peaks are home to ancient shadow beasts that occasionally emerge into the surface world.
