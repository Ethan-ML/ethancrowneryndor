Sanctuary of Leontrex

The sacred heart of Eryndor, where celestial light, ancient wisdom and unwavering guardians protect the balance of the world.

Discover Leontrex

Overview

Once one of the most sacred places in Eryndor, the Sanctuary of Leontrex was home to a civilization deeply connected to celestial magic. Its temples were built among luminous forests and quiet lakes, blending pale stone, silver ornamentation and natural elements into structures meant to exist in harmony with the land rather than dominate it.

That civilization no longer exists. The inhabitants of Leontrex were annihilated, leaving Asterion and the surrounding settlements abandoned. What remains are silent ruins, ancient sanctuaries and traces of a culture that once dedicated itself to protecting celestial knowledge and maintaining the magical balance of the region.

Sylvent is the last survivor of Leontrex and the only guardian who remains. His soul is intertwined with the sanctuary itself, binding his existence to the land he protects. From the main sanctuary, he watches over its ruins and prevents Morzeth, King of Gemora, from claiming or destroying the power still hidden there.

Despite the destruction of its people, Leontrex is far from lifeless. Silver-leaved trees, pale flowers and plants influenced by celestial energy continue to grow around its forests and midnight lagoons. White deer, luminous butterflies, celestial birds and other peaceful magical creatures still wander through the sanctuary, becoming some of the last living remnants of what Leontrex once was.
